Low Roll

Pick up the dice that land high. Lowest score wins.

Players

How to play
  • Everyone gets the same 13 dice: a d20, d12, d10, two d8s, a d4 and seven d6s.
  • Shake your phone to roll (or hold the Roll button and let go).
  • After every roll, pick up at least one die. Tap dice to choose.
  • A picked-up die scores how far it is below its max. A 20 on the d20 scores 0. A 10 on the d20 scores 10.
  • Re-roll whatever's left until every die is picked up. That's your score.
  • Pass the phone. Lowest total wins, highest total loses.
